\subsection{National Standards for Graduate Students}

The Philippine Qualifications Framework (PQF) Level 7 descriptors requires from the graduates of the master's degree programs with the following capacabilities:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Knowledge, Skills, and Values}

Demonstrated advanced knowledge and skills in a specialized or multi-disciplinary field of study for professional practice, self-directed research and/or lifelong learning.
\item \textbf{Application}

Applied in professional/creative work or research that requires self-direction and/or leadership in a specialized or multi-disciplinary professional work/research.
\item \textbf{Degree of Independence}

High substantial degree of independence that involves exercise of leadership and initiative individual work or in teams of multi-disciplinary field.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Other Components}

\subsection{Admission Requirements}

The admission requirements shall apply as prescribed in the Graduate Studies Philosophical Framework, and other latest policies or guidelines.

In addition to this, the Department of Computer Science shall also impose entry requirements for the following situations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The default source of input to the program are graduates of the computer or computing-related baccalaureate degree programs that provide substantial knowledge in the following areas:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Applicants must demonstrate proficiency in at least one (1) high-level programming language.
\item Applicants must have a general knowledge in Computer Science equivalent to the following: Discrete Mathematics, Algorithm Design and Analysis, Operating Systems, Programming Languages, Database Systems, and Software Engineering.
\end{enumerate}

\item Applicants with unsatisfactory academic background may be conditionally admitted to the program subject to applicable existing rules or policies, and be required to take appropriate bridging courses over and above to what is required in MSCS program.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Transferees from Within and Other MSU Campuses}

The admission of transferees shall apply as prescribed in the Graduate Studies Philosophical Framework, and other latest policies or guidelines.

\subsection{Retention Policies}

The retention policies shall apply as prescribed in the Graduate Studies Philosophical Framework, and other latest policies or guidelines.

\subsection{Fees and Other Payments}

The fees and other payments shall apply as prescribed in the Graduate Studies Philosophical Framework, and other latest policies or guidelines.

\subsection{Strategies and Processes Involved in the Revision}

The Department have undergone the following strategies and processes in coming up this revised MSCS program:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Examined the requirements for MSCS as prescribed in the CHED Memorandum Order (CMO) 7, series of 2010 entitled \textit{Revised Policies, Standards, and Guidelines for Graduate Program Information Technology Education (ITE)}
\item Examined the requirements of a graduate program as prescribed in the CHED Memorandum Order (CMO) 15, series of 2019 entitled  \textit{Policies, Standards, and Guidelines for Graduate Programs}
\item Inspected the Computer Science graduate curriculum of some popular higher education institutions local and abroad
\item Integrated the quality assurance requirements for outcomes-based education as prescribed in the CHED Memorandum Order (CMO) 46, series of 2012 entitled \textit{Policy-Standard to Enhance Quality Assurance (QA) in Phillippine Higher Education Through an Outcomes-Based and Typology-Based QA} 
\item Complied with the requirements of the MSU System for the revision of curriculum for both undergraduate and graduate programs
\item Conducted a series of meetings and informal discussions with Faculty members for the needed reforms and new directions of the MSCS program
\item Conducted a series of consultations and review with the representatives from the various departments of College of Computer Studies
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Implementation Scheme}

Some important information must be observed during the implementation of the program:

\begin{enumerate}
\item All courses are delivered through lecture sessions. The lecture sessions may require laboratory activities and students are allowed to use the open laboratory when necessary.
\item Students are allowed to take appropriate graduate courses related their project or research undertakings with supervisor's consent.
\item The thesis requirement of the program must be supervised by a Faculty of Computer Science
\item Existing rules or policies of the Graduate School shall apply to any situation or scenario during implementation.
\item The specialization courses required for each student shall be determined by the Program Advisor through its Program of Study.
\item The comprehensive examination shall be given after the student have taken the 60\% of the total course requirements of the program and prior to taking the thesis course. The exam shall be preferrably held on the later part of the first semester of the second year.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Budget Plan}

The revision of this program will not incur cost to this University. The only cost required is the regular maintenance of the classroom and laboratory facilities or equipment, including the office supplies needed in both classrooms and laboratories.

the rows representing the courses organized by Knowledge Areas (KAs). The map indicates the
extent to which the courses in the curriculum correspond to the student outcomes. The following
are the descriptions used to correlate the outcomes and the courses:

\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{L}] \textbf{Learned} in the course
\item[\textbf{P}] \textbf{Practiced} in the course with supervision
\item[\textbf{D}] \textbf{Demonstrated} in the course without supervision
\end{itemize}
